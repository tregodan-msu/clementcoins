The Isthmia archives

Clement Coin Catalog is one of a family of archives that put the records of the excavations at Isthmia online: the photographs, finds, notebooks and catalogs made by the excavation teams over more than half a century. Each archive keeps its own collection; the combined site searches all of them at once.

The archives

  • Clement Coin Catalog (this site) — Browse and search the coins from the UCLA/OSU/MSU excavations at Isthmia catalogued by Paul A. Clement: obverse and reverse photographs, mints, dates, weights and find contexts.
  • Gregory Archive — Browse, search and visualize the Timothy E. Gregory slide archive: 31,000+ photographs of Greece, Byzantium and the Mediterranean.
  • Isthmia Archives — searches all the archives at once
